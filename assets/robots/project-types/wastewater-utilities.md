## Wastewater Line Item Examples

Use HMS item naming style.

Examples:

```text
8" diameter, SDR 35 PVC gravity sewer pipe including fittings
6" diameter, HDPE force main including fused fittings
4' diameter, Precast concrete manhole with frame and cover
2" diameter, Combination air release valve in precast vault
5 HP, Submersible wastewater pump with guide rails and controls
480V/3PH, Duplex lift station control panel with telemetry
Wastewater bypass pumping system including setup and monitoring
```

Use `LOT`, not `LS`.

## Alaska-Specific Considerations

For Alaska wastewater projects, consider:

- Frozen ground excavation
- High groundwater
- Permafrost or poor soils
- Dewatering difficulty
- Remote equipment mobilization
- Barge or ferry delivery of pipe and structures
- Air freight for controls or replacement parts
- Limited aggregate sources
- Imported bedding material
- Waste backhaul
- Short construction season
- Temporary bypass during weather delays
- Heat trace and freeze protection
- Enclosures for controls and valves
- Remote startup and commissioning travel

## Pricing Notes

Wastewater pricing should include:

- Material base cost
- Freight to project site
- Handling and staging
- Labor
- Excavation and backfill
- Equipment
- Testing
- Startup
- Commissioning
- Controls integration
- General Requirements

Do not price pipe only as material. Installed utility pricing must include trenching, bedding, backfill, compaction, testing, and restoration unless those are clearly separated into other lines.

---

## Common Clarifications

Ask or flag:

- Is bypass pumping required?
- Are existing utilities active during construction?
- Are manholes coated or uncoated?
- Are fittings included in pipe unit price?
- Is rock excavation included or excluded?
- Is contaminated soil expected?
- Are service connections included?
- Are controls vendor-provided or contractor-provided?
- Is SCADA programming included?
- Is operator training required?
- Are startup services included with equipment vendor quotes?

## Assumption Examples

```text
ASSUMPTION: Pipe unit cost includes fittings, trenching, bedding, backfill, compaction, and testing.
ASSUMPTION: Bypass pumping included as budgetary LOT due to incomplete sequencing information.
VERIFY: Existing sewer service continuity requirements not defined.
VERIFY: SCADA point count and programming responsibility not provided.
EXCLUSION: Rock excavation excluded unless specifically shown.
```

## Quality Control

Before finalizing a wastewater estimate, check:

- Pipe lengths by size and material
- Manhole counts
- Service counts
- Trench quantities
- Bedding and backfill
- Bypass pumping
- Dewatering
- Surface restoration
- Controls / SCADA
- Electrical support
- Testing and commissioning
- Freight and remote logistics
- Alternates and phasing
- General Requirements

The estimate should clearly state what is included in each utility unit price and what is priced separately.
