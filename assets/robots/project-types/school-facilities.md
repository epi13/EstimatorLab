This guide is intended for Alaska school projects, including municipal, borough, school district, and state-funded work.

## Alaska-Specific Considerations

For Alaska school projects, consider:

- Summer construction windows
- Occupied school phasing
- Work around school calendar
- Rural school logistics
- Remote freight and material lead times
- Limited local subcontractor availability
- Temporary heat during shoulder seasons
- Delivery timing before school startup
- Security and access restrictions
- High cost of remobilization for missed items
- Specialty subcontractor travel

---

## Renovation and Occupied Facility Notes

School renovations often require additional cost for:

- Phasing
- Temporary protection
- Dust barriers
- Negative air or containment where required
- Off-hours work
- Weekend work
- Temporary signage and egress
- Coordination with staff and students
- Floor leveling after demolition
- Patching existing walls and ceilings
- Matching existing finishes
- Moving furniture or equipment
- Final cleaning

Do not price school renovations as open new construction unless the building is fully vacant and unrestricted.

## VE Review Notes

School projects often include VE review for:

- Finish substitutions
- Flooring changes
- Ceiling changes
- Casework reductions
- Door and hardware changes
- Lighting fixture substitutions
- HVAC equipment revisions
- Exterior envelope simplification
- Sitework reductions
- Alternate phasing

For VE items, always identify original scope, proposed change, add cost, deduct cost, and net impact.

## Common Clarifications

Ask or flag:

- Is the building occupied during construction?
- Is work limited to summer break?
- Are after-hours or weekend shifts required?
- Are hazardous materials present?
- Are existing finishes to be patched or replaced full-room?
- Are door hardware sets fully defined?
- Are access control and security devices included?
- Are low-voltage/data drops included?
- Are temporary classrooms required?
- Is owner-furnished equipment included or excluded?

---

## Assumption Examples

```text
ASSUMPTION: Pricing assumes summer construction during school break with normal weekday shifts.
ASSUMPTION: Interior renovation pricing includes minor patching at disturbed adjacent finishes.
VERIFY: Hazardous materials abatement scope not provided.
VERIFY: Access control hardware requirements not fully defined.
EXCLUSION: Owner-furnished classroom furniture excluded unless noted.
```

## Quality Control

Before finalizing a school facility estimate, check:

- Phasing and schedule assumptions
- Occupied facility impacts
- Demolition and patching
- Hazardous materials
- Doors, frames, and hardware
- Interior finishes
- Casework and specialties
- Fire alarm and life safety
- Low-voltage systems
- HVAC controls and balancing
- Temporary protection
- Final cleaning
- Sitework and playground impacts
- Freight and Alaska logistics
- General Requirements

The estimate should clearly state whether work assumes an occupied school, summer shutdown, or unrestricted construction access.
