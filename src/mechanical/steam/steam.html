Notes / Logic
Steam installs are more fabrication-heavy: rigging, code-rated safeties, feedwater/condensate handling, blowdown cooling, and controls. We assume an boiler skid, local code safeties, feed tank or DA skid, basic condensate tie-in, and startup/combustion tuning.
Material pricing is multiplied by remoteFactor. Shipping/handling is a % of that, representing barge / crate / special handling in Alaska. Labor is Anchorage mechanical rate ($92/hr) with a live-facility premium toggle.
This is still budgetary. Final submittal needs stamped mechanical design and AHJ approval.
