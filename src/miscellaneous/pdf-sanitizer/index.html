Blueprint Sanitizer

Crop title blocks, black out fine details, and export a flattened PDF with the source document metadata and hidden layers removed.

How it works: choose the page to clean, then drag a crop box around the portion to keep or drag blackout boxes over small identifiers. Every exported page is rasterized, so searchable text, annotations, embedded files, scripts, and document metadata are not carried into the new PDF.
