    ⚠️ OSHA Trench Safety Requirements

    Trenches deeper than 5 feet require a protective system unless excavated entirely in stable rock. Sloped trenches at a 2:1 ratio (horizontal:vertical) are standard for Type C soils. Always consult OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P and local regulations for compliance requirements.
